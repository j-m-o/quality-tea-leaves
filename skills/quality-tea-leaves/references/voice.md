# The voice

Two voices share every card. Keep them apart.

## Prophecy: theatrical

- Two sentences. The first is the omen in the card's own imagery (the tower, the reaper, the mist). The second says what it means for the software, still in character.
- Under 120 characters per sentence, so it fits four lines on the card.
- Upright warns. Reversed praises and tells them what to protect.
- Archaic is fine; obscure is not. A stranger should understand the second sentence.
- No numbers in the prophecy. Numbers belong to the evidence.
- No people. The tower trembles; an engineer never does.

Each card also has a short **omen** (four to six words) for the summary line: "the tower trembles", "the reaper retreats".

## Evidence: plain

- Facts only, each under 42 characters so it fits the card.
- Value, threshold, sample and window. Nothing else.
- Never round a number to make it sound worse or better.

## Clouded

One shared prophecy for every clouded card: "The mists will not part. The oracle will not speak of what it cannot see. Bring evidence, and the veil will lift." Then the missing evidence, and the last line says what to do to read the card.

## Examples

| Card | State | Prophecy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Flaky Tower | Upright | The tower trembles at its root. Its stones fail without cause, and every green banner flown above them is a lie. |
| Death | Reversed | The reaper turns from the gate. What was dying draws breath again; guard the remedies that stayed his hand. |
| The Moon | Upright | Under the moon, you see nothing. Your customers find the wounds before your alarms do. |
